# Construction Procurement Tender Platform

The tender platform is a multifunctional online system that connects clients, construction companies, craftsmen, carriers, owners of special equipment, day laborers, and construction material suppliers. 
The main goal of the platform is to simplify the process of placing orders, receiving offers, comparing prices, and selecting a contractor in the construction and service sectors. 
The client no longer needs to search separately for a craftsman, transport, equipment, labor, or material suppliers on different sites or social networks. 
All necessary services are gathered in one system.

## How the Platform Works
The client registers on the platform, selects the appropriate category, and fills out a job or service application. 
The application can include: 
* Job description; 
* Job category; 
* Object location; 
* Estimated budget; 
* Desired completion deadline; 
* Scope of work; 
* Required number of workers; 
* Required equipment; 
* List of materials; 
* Photos; 
* PDF or JPG format projects; 
* Additional conditions and notes. 

After publishing the application, contractors in the corresponding category can see the order and offer their price and execution conditions. This creates a competitive environment where the client can receive offers from various contractors and compare prices, deadlines, and terms.

## Tender Principle
One of the main features of the platform is price competition. 
Contractors send their offers for the same job. 
The offer may include: 
* Job price; 
* Execution deadline; 
* Job start date; 
* Material prices; 
* Transportation costs; 
* Additional conditions. 

The client compares the offers, which are listed from the lowest price, and makes the final choice of contractor; the platform never declares a winner automatically. 
The client may choose at any time — before the deadline, which closes the tender early, or after it, within a selection window (14 days by default). If no offer is chosen in that window, the tender expires. If the agreement with the chosen contractor is declined, the client may choose again among the remaining offers. 
The goal of this principle is to keep the process transparent: every participant competes in the same open comparison, and the decision belongs to the client.

## Application Viewing System
Before viewing the full application, the contractor can only see the basic information: 
* Job category; 
* Short description of the work; 
* Budget; 
* Country and city; 
* Job location; 
* Publication date. 

The client's personal information, phone number, and other contact details are hidden. 
To see the full information, paid access can be used. 
According to the defined model: 
* Viewing one application — $1; 
* Monthly package — $20; 
* Annual package — $200. 

Sending an offer is free if the contractor has already gained access to the application.

## Application Statuses
An application and job go through several stages: 
1. Draft; 
2. Published application; 
3. Active tender; 
4. Receiving offers; 
5. Pending agreement; 
6. Agreed; 
7. Work in progress; 
8. Work completed; 
9. Evaluated; 
10. Cancelled or expired. 

Both the client and the contractor can see the current status in their personal cabinet.

## Evaluation and Rating System
Leaving a review is only possible after a truly completed job. 
This means a user cannot evaluate a person or company with whom they have not had a confirmed job on the platform. 
The evaluation includes: 
* Star rating; 
* Quality of work; 
* Adherence to deadlines; 
* Communication; 
* Adherence to the agreed price; 
* Mandatory justified comment. 

Empty or unjustified reviews must not be published. 
This rule reduces the risk of fake reviews, artificially boosting ratings by acquaintances, and discrediting competitors.

## White and Black List
The platform includes an additional reliability control system.

### White List
A contractor who has successfully and on time completed at least five agreed jobs can get on the white list. 
A client can also get on the white list if they: 
* Pay the agreed amount on time; 
* Do not cancel work without reason; 
* Follow the platform's rules; 
* Cooperate in good faith.

### Black List
A user who has repeatedly failed to fulfill a confirmed agreement can get on the black list. 
For example: 
* Contractor did not start the agreed work; 
* Abandoned the job; 
* Did not fulfill set obligations; 
* Client did not pay the agreed amount; 
* User violated the agreement three times. 

The estimated duration of being on the black list could be one year. 
Any violation, restriction, or possible sanction must be known in advance and clearly shown to the user.

## Dispute Issue
The platform does not include a module for resolving financial or legal disputes between users. 
The platform does not act as a judge, arbitrator, or expert. 
Its functions are: 
* Placing an application; 
* Receiving offers; 
* Recording an agreement; 
* Managing job status; 
* Evaluating completed work; 
* Recording platform rule violations. 

Personal agreements made outside the platform are not considered by the system.
