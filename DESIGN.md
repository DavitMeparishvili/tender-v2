# Design System: Tender

## Overview

### Scoped tender surface extension

The approved tender detail and owned-tender list redesign uses compact white
panels on cool grey, darker emerald actions, and labelled project facts. Its
source is `src/modules/tenders/ui/tender-pages.css` and the shared tender heading,
sections, and list components. All `tender-*` tokens below belong to these
surfaces and their scoped editor and shell treatments. The following tender
subsections take precedence within that scope, including action text colour,
card shape, elevation, and responsive layout. Existing homepage, authentication,
discovery, wizard, and other surface guidance remains in force outside it.

**Creative North Star: "Live Supply, Honest Mechanism"**

Tender is a construction marketplace, and a marketplace earns trust by showing
what is actually open right now and by being explicit about the mechanism that
closes a tender. The interface leads with live work — open tenders, offer
counts, closing countdowns, category volumes — and states the rule that decides
the winner rather than implying negotiation.

The system is built on the Tipalti EU foundations: near-black ink and paper
carry the page, a single brand emerald marks the one thing to do next, and a
hairline grid motif signs every banded surface. Depth is soft and cool, never
glassy. Numbers are typeset as quantities, not as decoration.

**Key Characteristics:**

- Emerald is the single pop, used for the primary action, the active tab and the
  live countdown — never as a background for body text.
- A 1200px content column with 40px gutters governs every band, marketing and
  workspace alike.
- Cards are the unit of structure: hairline border, soft cool shadow, 20–28px
  radius, and a raise on hover only where the card is a destination.
- The grid motif is always masked with a fade, so it reads as a watermark
  rather than graph paper.
- Privacy and selection rules are visible in the UI, not buried in copy.

## Colors

### Scoped tender palette

Tender page, ink, muted, and line tokens supply the cool ground, strong text,
metadata, and separators. White panels remain distinct from that ground without
shadow. Tender green marks primary actions, section selection, focus, and map
details. White text is used on this darker tender green and its hover colour;
the same contrast-safe emerald is shared with the global brand token.
Status badges pair the tender status foreground and soft background, neutral
badges identify ownership and counts, and category badges use a cool blue pair
on list cards. Privacy notices use the tender privacy pair. Labels carry the
meaning alongside these colours.

### Primary

- **Brand Emerald (#047857):** the single accent. Primary actions, the active nav
  underline, countdowns, histogram fills, the lowest-offer highlight.
- **Tender Ink (#16181C):** headings, high-confidence text, the utility strip,
  the footer, and dark lanes.

### Neutral

The grey ramp is a true neutral from paper (#F8F8F9) to near-ink (#2B2D33).
`grey-200` is the default hairline; `grey-300` is the interactive border;
`grey-600` is muted text; `grey-700` is body copy.

### Secondary

- **Periwinkle (#8194DD):** category tags and links.
- **Mauve (#A664A6) with Pale Violet:** a second category tone.
- **Green (#466644) with Green Soft:** verified, open, available.
- **Red (#BA5147) with Red Soft:** repair and cancellation only.

### Named Rules

**The Contrast-Safe Emerald Rule.** Actions use white on #047857 (5.49:1),
with #065F46 on hover. On charcoal, emerald text uses the lighter #6EE7B7.
Secondary labels and placeholders use grey-600 on white or cool paper.

**The Single-Pop Rule.** One emerald element owns each decision. If two emerald
elements compete inside a card, one of them is wrong.

**The Scarce-Signal Rule.** Green means verified or open, red means repair.
Neither is decoration, and neither carries meaning by colour alone.

## Typography

### Shared roles and delivery

`app/typography.css` is the shared source for headings, body, labels, controls
and inputs. Georgian defaults to Noto Sans Georgian with Noto Sans fallback;
English and Russian put Noto Sans first, retaining Georgian fallback for mixed
records. The validated URL locale sets the document `lang`.

The locale layout imports Fontsource production WOFF2 faces at genuine
400/500/600/700 weights with `font-display: swap`. Noto Sans Unicode ranges
request only the subsets needed for rendered content. Fontsource retains the
upstream OFL licenses, also distributed in `public/fonts/licenses`.

- Hero: 44/52.8px desktop and 32/39px mobile, 700.
- Detail/list title: 32/40px desktop and 28/35px mobile, 700.
- Section: 24px desktop / 22px mobile, 1.35, 600.
- Card: 20px desktop / 18px mobile, 1.4, 600.
- Body: 16/26px, 400, with a maximum 65ch prose measure.
- Controls: 15px/1.4, 600; labels 13–14px/1.5, 400–500.
- Editable inputs: 16/24px, 400, with at least 44px height.
- Mobile bottom navigation is a compact exception: 12px, or 11px below 361px.
  Short translated labels fit without clipping; enlarged text reduces columns.

Normal Georgian tracking is zero. Category chips retain natural case; the
existing intentional uppercase overlines have Mtavruli coverage. Aligned
amounts and quantities use tabular numerals. The file-format icon's 8px lettering
is decorative; the adjacent readable filename supplies the content.

## Layout

### Scoped tender layout

The tender container has a 1200px maximum width and its own gutter tokens.
At 1100px the gutter narrows to the tablet value; at 760px it narrows to the
mobile value. Detail content pairs a flexible main column with a 320px rail
and the tender column gap; the rail becomes 280px between 761px and 1100px.
The four-column fact strip becomes two columns at 760px. Section panels use
the section gap and switch to the mobile gap in the collapsed detail layout.
Collapsed children stretch to the available width. Owner offer status moves
ahead of the sections; owner management follows them.

Desktop section links remain sticky below the header. Mobile section links
form a wrapping two-column grid in normal flow. Fixed task actions sit above
the measured bottom navigation and safe-area inset; between 641px and 760px
they sit directly at the viewport bottom. A ResizeObserver reserves the actual
height of both bars as fonts, locales and text size change. Owned-list search precedes wrapping
scrollable status tabs on mobile. Facts and card actions reflow rather than
shrinking the desktop card into the viewport. Photo grids change from four
columns to two. These rules replace the legacy tender-detail layout below only
for the scoped tender surfaces.

Every band centres a 1200px column with 40px gutters, narrowing to 24px below
1180px and 18px below 640px.

- **Landing:** full-width bands alternating white, grey-50, ink and brand.
- **Discovery:** a 296px filter rail beside `minmax(0, 1fr)` of results.
- **Tender detail:** `minmax(0, 1fr)` beside a 372px sticky rail.
- **Profile:** `minmax(0, 1fr)` beside a 340px sticky rail.

Below 1024px each two-column layout collapses to one. On tender detail the
unlock card moves **above** the description, because the unlock is the decision
the page exists to support. Below 640px a five-item bottom tab bar appears and
the header nav tabs hide; the tab bar's centre "Post" action is a 40px emerald
circle offset 8px upward.

**The One-Column Rule.** A collapsed layout reorders by decision priority, not
by source order.

## Elevation & Depth

### Scoped tender depth

Tender section, fact, owner-list, and sidebar cards are flat white surfaces
with the tender line border. Existing sidebar shadows are removed within this
scope. There is no hover raise on these cards. Hierarchy comes from the cool
page ground, separators, spacing, and tinted status or privacy regions.

Shadows are soft and cool-tinted (`rgb(11 27 59 / …)`), five steps from `xs` to
`xl`. A card at rest uses `xs` or `sm`; a card that is a destination raises to
`md` or `lg` on hover and takes an `emerald-300` border. The hero preview card and
the mobile filter sheet are the only surfaces at `xl`.

**The Raise-Means-Reachable Rule.** A hover raise promises a destination. A
card that does not navigate does not raise.

## Shapes

### Scoped tender shapes

Tender panels and the fact strip use the tender panel radius; rectangular
actions use the control radius, search/sort fields use the field radius, and
attachments use the attachment radius. Status badges retain their own rounded
capsule shape. Mobile fixed actions have rounded top corners matching panels.
These scoped controls and cards replace the global pill and large-card shape
rules below on tender surfaces.

Radii run 4 / 6 / 10 / 14 / 20 / 28 and the pill. Controls and inputs use 10px;
result and category cards use 14–20px; feature cards and the unlock gate use
28px; every button and chip is a pill. Icon chips are 42px squares at 11px
radius on an emerald-soft ground.

## Components

### Scoped tender components

- **Actions:** primary and secondary variants share a 44px minimum height,
  compact padding, and the tender action role. Primary hover darkens the
  emerald; secondary hover uses the tender control-hover ground and line.
  Disabled buttons have 0.55 opacity and a not-allowed cursor. Focus on buttons,
  links, fields, and disclosure summaries uses a 2px tender-green outline with
  a 3px offset. Small document actions use the explicit compact variant.
- **Section panels and facts:** white, flat, hairline-bordered panels group
  overview, quantities, attachments, and location. Padding reduces to 16px on
  mobile. A semantic definition list pairs fact labels with strong tabular
  values; separators follow its responsive grid.
- **Badges:** labelled status and neutral variants share the badge token.
  Category badges are smaller uppercase labels; they are omitted from compact
  owner cards where the title and status take priority.
- **Search and navigation:** the owned-list search is a bordered field beside
  the sort control on desktop. Status selection combines tint and a count
  badge on desktop, then an underline on mobile. Section navigation marks the
  targeted section with an emerald underline and foreground.
- **Attachments and privacy:** document rows combine a file-type mark, wrapping
  filename, metadata, and access-appropriate actions. Photos use a four-to-two
  column grid. Privacy notices remain paired with explanatory text; rendered
  file and location details depend on server-provided access.
- **Empty and unavailable states:** empty lists and offer panels use explicit
  text. Owner edit availability follows the current server state, with disabled
  controls and explanatory copy where editing is unavailable. Details and
  disclosure actions stay keyboard reachable when available.

### Buttons

- **Shape:** pill, minimum 44px target, 11px/18px padding.
- **Brand:** emerald ground, ink text, `xs` shadow; hover `#047857`, active
  `#065F46`. No scale change on press.
- **Outline:** paper ground, `grey-300` border, ink text; hover border to ink.
- **Ink:** used where emerald would compete with an emerald element already present.
- **Pending:** the action label stays visible with lower-emphasis status copy
  appended; the control never collapses to a spinner.

### Inputs / Fields

Paper ground, `grey-300` border, 10px radius, 44px minimum height. Focus takes
an emerald border plus the 4px soft focus ring. Error takes a red border with
specific repair copy below, and the form-level summary links back to each
field. Disabled controls take the grey-50 ground — used for "Specific service"
before a category is chosen.

### Navigation

A 34px ink utility strip carries the market statement, help and the locale
switch. Below it a 68px white nav (sticky, 86% white with a 10px blur) carries
the wordmark, the destination tabs and the primary action. The active tab takes
a 2px emerald bottom border and 700 weight. Destinations are built from the
viewer's capabilities in one place, so a route the server would deny is never
offered as navigation.

### Cards and Figures

A figure is a 11.5px muted label above a 700-weight tabular value. Four figures
above a hairline is the standard strip for tender and profile summaries. The
hairline field grid (1px gaps over a `grey-200` ground) is used where empty
values must still read as fields; an empty value renders "Not provided" in
`grey-400` and stays programmatically associated with its label.

### The Unlock Gate

The unlock card is the only surface with a 1.5px emerald border. It carries an
emerald-soft header with a lock icon, the verbatim access copy, one emerald primary
action, a labelled divider, and the two subscription options as outlined rows.
The simulated-payment note is always present and never softened.

### Countdown

Counters tick once per second and read `Nd Nh` above a day, `Nh NNm` above an
hour, and `Nm NNs` below it. The first value is rendered on the server so the
markup is correct before hydration. A passed deadline switches the tender to
its closed presentation and stops the timer.

### Reviews

A review is presented only against a verified completed engagement. The
summary is a 52px score, an emerald star string, a five-row histogram with 7px
tracks, and the four per-criteria scores. Star strings always carry a text
equivalent ("4.8 out of 5, 61 reviews").

### Map

The map is a control, not a canvas: one hairline border, 14px radius, inside
the detail card. Raster basemap tiles are muted (lightly desaturated, not
greyscale) so their natural greens, blues and road tones stay legible while the
marks on top still read as the loudest thing on the card. A precise location
is an ink circular pin with a white ring; an
approximate location is an emerald area circle and never a pin. Attribution is
always visible.

**The Quiet-Basemap Rule.** The basemap is context, not content.

**The Coarse-Circle Rule.** A map shown before entitlement draws an area, never
a point, and that area is sized from the real coordinate coarsening.

**The Map-Is-Enhancement Rule.** A map never becomes the only way to complete a
task; the coordinate fields stay visible and functional beside it.

## Motion

Short fades and small translate-ups on scroll-in, 200–300ms ease-out. Colour
and border transitions are 150ms. No bounce, no looping motion on content. The
only continuous mark in the system is the pulsing dot on the live-offer toast,
and it is decorative and `aria-hidden`.

## Do's and Don'ts

For the scoped tender surfaces, use the prefixed tokens and tender subsections
above whenever an older global visual rule conflicts. Preserve actual record
values, permission boundaries, labelled statuses, and bilingual content.

### Do:

- **Do** show real supply: real open tenders, real counts, real reviews.
- **Do** keep every number tabular and every number labelled.
- **Do** reserve emerald for the single next action on the surface.
- **Do** state the selection rule wherever offers are shown.
- **Do** test Georgian and English at 1280px, 1024px and 390px.

### Don't:

- **Don't** put white text on emerald, anywhere, in any state.
- **Don't** invent tenders, offers, ratings, revenue or statistics to fill a
  section. An empty section says it is empty; a placeholder says it is a
  placeholder.
- **Don't** reveal client identity, exact location, attachments or conditions
  before an entitlement — the projection is enforced on the server, never by
  hiding markup on the client.
- **Don't** use the grid motif unmasked, or at a density that competes with
  content.
- **Don't** communicate status by colour alone.

### Offer status colours

Offer badges retain explicit text labels. Won uses `--trust` on `--trust-soft`; not selected uses `--error` on `--error-soft`. Awaiting deadline/result uses `--status-pending-text` (#854d0e) on `--status-pending-soft` (#fef3c7), a restrained amber with dark readable text. Withdrawn, cancelled and ended use `--fg-2` on `--bg-subtle`. Use the same mapping on the offer list and detail.
