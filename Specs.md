# Project Specifications: tender-marketplace

* **Main Page:** Registration, Login, Listings, Statistics, White/Black list, Advertisements.
* **Roles:** Client, Construction/Repair Contractor, Transporter, Equipment Owner, Daily Worker, Administrator.
* **Categories:** Construction/Repair, Transport, Equipment, Daily Worker, Materials Procurement, and others.
* **Client Dashboard:** Upload listing, PDF/JPG projects, Manage listings, Received offers.
* **Contractor Dashboard:** View listings, Paid access, Send free offer, Work history.
* **Transport:** Start/End address, Map marking, and real road distance.
* **Tender Principle:** The client compares the offers (listed from the lowest price) and chooses the contractor; the system never picks a winner automatically. The client may choose at any time, before or after the deadline; if no offer is chosen within the selection window (14 days after the deadline), the tender expires. If the agreement with the chosen contractor is declined or not confirmed in time, the client chooses again among the remaining offers.
* **Payments:** Posting a listing is free; Viewing one listing is $1, Monthly package $20, Yearly $200.
* **Agreement Stages:** Active → Awaiting agreement → Agreed → In progress → Completed.
* **Ratings/Reviews:** Only after completed and confirmed work, mandatory justified comment.
* **Disputes Module:** None.
* **Verification:** By email or phone; ID document for additional trustworthiness.
* **Administrator:** Users, Listings, Payments, Revenues, and Financial Report.
* **Technology:** Next.js, TypeScript, Prisma, and Database.
* **Main Project Name:** tender-marketplace
